Senior Project

Senior Project · KMITL · 2026

Reinforcement Learning for Process Control and Embedded Systems

Developed a reinforcement learning–based control system to replace conventional PID controllers for process control applications.

The system was designed and implemented on a real embedded platform, enabling the RL agent to interact directly with physical processes in real time. A Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) algorithm was applied to learn optimal control policies under dynamic conditions.

The proposed approach demonstrates improved adaptability and reduced manual tuning compared to traditional control methods, highlighting the potential of integrating AI with industrial control systems.
